# Prior art registry

The bounded external-repository corpus reconstructed through 2026-09-04 is
recorded in the machine-readable
[`sources/external-projects.json`](sources/external-projects.json) ledger and
the human-readable [`docs/external-projects.md`](docs/external-projects.md)
view. It covers current-kit tools and authorities plus direct sources found in
the supplied setup inventory, sanitized historical dependency declarations,
local extension records, and research notes. Each record states the
relationship, access date, and license; reference-only sources are not copied.

This is an evidence-bounded attribution registry, not a claim that every tool
ever present on a workstation was recovered. Transitive packages, private
repository identities, and historical application source and prose are out of
scope. Add newly discovered direct sources rather than silently widening the
claim.

Vendor product documentation is recorded separately in
[`sources/official-docs.json`](sources/official-docs.json). A citation grants
neither trust nor permission to redistribute source code.

For new prior art, record the canonical URL, reviewed revision when one exists,
license, exact borrowing scope, evidence, and redistribution decision before
copying or adapting anything.
